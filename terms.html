01. Welcome & agreement

Thank you for considering a commission from acocy arts! These terms describe how personal-use digital art commissions work. Before you pay, we will confirm your brief, exact price, timeline, delivery method, and any additional conditions in writing.

By confirming the commission order after receiving the terms, you agree to the agreed terms, without affecting any mandatory consumer rights. Sending an enquiry is not an order.

02. Artwork & quotes

Available styles are Sketch, Flat Colour, and Rendered, in Bust-up, Half-body, or Full-body sizes. The prices displayed on this website are starting prices for one character with a simple or plain background.

Complicated details, additional characters, special backgrounds or substantial extra work may require a personalised quote. No extra fee will be charged without your approval. The artist may decline a request before payment.

03. Content boundaries

The artist accepts general safe-for-work requests and may consider mild, non-explicit suggestive artwork depending on the subject and comfort level. Acceptance is always at the artist’s discretion.

The artist does not accept explicit sexual content, sexualized depictions of minors, hateful or exploitative content, or other requests outside her boundaries. She will clarify whether a request can be accepted before taking payment.

04. Payment

Payment is arranged directly with the artist through PayPal Goods & Services, not on this website. The total approved quote is split into 50% before work begins and 50% after the final preview is approved, before delivery. The artist begins after the first payment is confirmed.

All displayed prices are in euros (EUR). Any applicable transaction costs included in the order price or payable by the customer will be disclosed before the order is confirmed. Never send money until the artist has accepted the commission and supplied verified payment instructions.

05. Artwork process & revisions

The typical process is: enquiry → confirmed quote → first payment → sketch preview → agreed revisions → completion and watermarked preview → second payment → final PNG delivery.

Two reasonable revision requests during the sketch stage are included. Please provide references and all important details early. Material changes to an approved sketch or to the original brief may require extra payment or time, both agreed beforehand. Errors where the piece does not match the agreed brief are handled appropriately and are not subject to an arbitrary revision fee.

06. Timing & communication

An estimated completion date or timeframe is supplied and agreed before payment. More complex requests or revisions may affect that estimate. If there is a significant delay, the artist will contact the customer and discuss available remedies, including those required by applicable law.

Please answer questions and approve stages within a reasonable time. Silence is not automatically treated as approval. If communication pauses, the artist will contact you to agree how to proceed.

07. Final delivery

Final art is sent as a high-resolution PNG through a delivery channel agreed with the customer, after full payment is confirmed. The preview before the final payment may be watermarked or reduced-resolution.

The size/resolution and delivery channel should be confirmed in advance if you need something specific. Please save your delivered file safely.

08. Personal use & copyright

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the artist retains the copyright in her original artwork. The client receives permission to use the delivered illustration for personal, non-commercial purposes, such as a personal profile image, wallpaper or non-monetized social media post. Credit to acocy is appreciated when sharing.

You may not sell the image, place it on merchandise, claim authorship, use it as a business logo, licence it to others, mint NFTs from it, or use it for training AI models. Commercial licensing is not currently offered. Any pre-existing characters, designs and other third-party rights remain with their respective owners.

09. Portfolio permission & privacy

The artist will ask your permission before sharing your commissioned artwork publicly in a portfolio or on social media. If you say no, it will not be used as promotional content. Private messages, identifying details, and references are not posted without appropriate permission.

The artist may show separate work that is independently hers, such as her own character studies, without representing it as customer-approved commissioned work.

10. Cancellation, refunds & legal rights

If you need to cancel, contact the artist promptly. Refunds and payments for work performed will be considered based on the stage of work, what was agreed, and applicable law. A deposit is not automatically non-refundable in every circumstance. If the artist cancels or cannot deliver, she will discuss an appropriate remedy and refund as required.

Consumer rights, including any applicable withdrawal rights, rights relating to defective or undelivered digital content, and payment-provider dispute rights, are not excluded by these terms. Do not assume all custom digital art commissions automatically waive the EU/EEA withdrawal period: the rules depend on the contract classification and any required consent, information and acknowledgement. The artist will provide legally required information before a paid order begins.

11. Questions

Questions or requests to change or cancel an existing commission should be sent through the artist’s verified social account or the contact channel agreed for that order. This website does not collect, process or accept payments or commission requests directly.

Please keep a copy of the confirmed quote and message history for reference.

These terms are a working draft and need to match the artist’s actual process and any mandatory local consumer rules before they are used for paid orders.
