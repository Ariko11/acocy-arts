06 / a few questions

good to know

How do I pay?

After your request is accepted, payment is arranged directly via PayPal Goods & Services. Pay 50% before the drawing begins and 50% after the final preview is approved, before receiving the finished PNG. There is no website checkout.

How many changes can I request?

Two reasonable revision requests are included during the sketch stage. Larger changes after approval may need a new quote. Mistakes against the agreed brief will be corrected as appropriate, without taking away your legal rights.

Can I use the art for my business?

Not at this time. Commissions are licensed for personal, non-commercial use only. Commercial licensing is currently unavailable.

Will my artwork be posted online?

Only if you give permission. I'll ask before sharing your commissioned artwork in my portfolio or on social media.

Can I request suggestive art?

Mild, non-explicit suggestive artwork may be considered at the artist's discretion. Explicit sexual content, sexualized minors, hateful content and other requests outside the artist's boundaries are not accepted.
