Information needed before public launch

This is a launch checklist, not a completed legal notice. Depending on where the artist actually operates and the legal nature of the activity, a published business/imprint notice may need the legal provider name, a serviceable contact address, relevant registration details, contact information and other required disclosures.

Do not invent any of these details or publish private personal information casually. Have the artist confirm her operating jurisdiction and what details are legally required, then replace this checklist with the verified notice.

Seller / provider

Pending artist confirmation: legally required operator name or business name, address (where required), country of establishment, and a contact method beyond a social handle if applicable.

Registration and regulatory details

Pending verification: whether any applicable registration, trade licence, tax identification, professional authority or consumer-information disclosures need to be shown. This depends on where and how the commissions are sold.

Consumer disclosures & website operation

Before accepting orders, have the required pre-contract information, withdrawal/cancellation process, privacy details and an accurate legal notice reviewed for the seller’s circumstances. The public site can stay payment-free, but selling commissions through social media may still create legal obligations.
