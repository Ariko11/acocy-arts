Artwork ownership

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, original commissioned illustrations are created by acocy, and she retains copyright in her own original contributions. Commission fees pay for the artwork and the stated licence, not automatic copyright transfer.

If an illustration includes a customer-created character, fan character or third-party content, any underlying rights remain with the relevant rights holders. The artist does not claim ownership of your original character merely because she draws it.

What personal use includes

Customers may use delivered art as a personal profile picture, desktop/phone wallpaper, or a personal, non-commercial social media post. Please credit acocy where practical.

What is not licensed

Without additional permission, artworks may not be resold, redistributed as downloadable assets, used for merchandise or business branding, offered for commercial licensing, used to create NFTs, or used as material for AI-model training. Commercial licences are not offered at this time.

Portfolio and sharing permissions

acocy will ask before posting a client commission on her social channels or public portfolio. Clients can decline. This does not stop the artist from presenting her own separate non-commission artwork.

Copyright questions

If you believe something displayed on this website infringes your rights, please reach out via the verified social accounts once published. Explain what content is involved and your concern so it can be reviewed.
