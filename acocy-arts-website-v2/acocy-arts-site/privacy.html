What this website does

This is a static informational website. It currently has no checkout, no account registration, no newsletter, no advertising pixels and no analytics scripts. The commission-request builder runs in your browser. It does not send your selections or notes to acocy or to a server.

What happens when you copy a request

When you select a size and finish, the website generates a message locally. Pressing “copy commission request” asks your browser to copy that message to your clipboard. To make an enquiry, you must separately send it through a communication service of your choice.

Third parties and technical logs

If you follow a social-media link or use PayPal outside this website, those services may process data under their own privacy notices. The hosting provider may process standard technical data (such as IP addresses and access logs) to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. The exact hosting provider and legal details must be verified before launch.

Commission communication

When you contact the artist through social media or another agreed channel, she may need your username, reference images, order details and payment records to manage the commission. Any legally required privacy information about this separate processing should be supplied before taking orders. Sensitive personal data is not required for a normal commission request.

Privacy requests

For data access, correction, deletion or related privacy requests, use the verified social account or the order contact channel. A dedicated privacy contact and accurate controller details should be added here before the site is published.
